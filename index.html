01About Full page →

Built for Africa. Trusted by the world's best.

Leveraging Akita Group's integrated "Logistics + Trade + Investment" resource triangle, the company has been deeply engaged in the African logistics market for over a decade — with a localized service network covering Guinea, Sierra Leone, the Republic of the Congo, Djibouti and other African countries, focused on minerals, new energy, oversized equipment and project logistics.

To date, Akita Group has built deep partnerships with major central SOEs, mining groups and shipping lines — providing reliable supply-chain support for China–Africa infrastructure construction, equipment export and resource transportation.

  • Ship-agency licenses in Guinea, Sierra Leone and Congo
  • Priority berthing at 3 key ports
  • Official COSCO designated agent — Guinea
  • Authorized Sailun Tires agent — Guinea
  • Shaanxi Auto official parts warehouse
  • Customs clearance ≈ 40% faster than industry average
  • 24/7 cargo visibility & tracking
