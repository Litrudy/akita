04FAQ Placeholder answers — to validate

The questions we always get.

How fast can you clear cargo in Conakry?+

Timing depends on the cargo type and how complete the documentation is. Indicative lead times per country and regime will be published here.

Do you handle dangerous goods and oversized cargo?+

Oversized is our specialty — up to 110 t per piece on our multi-axle low-beds. For dangerous goods, contact us with the MSDS for case-by-case confirmation.

Which documents do I need to import into Guinea?+

Typically: bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list and, depending on the product, specific certificates. A detailed per-country checklist will be published here.

Can you quote door-to-door from China?+

Yes — our Shenzhen desk coordinates pickup and export in China, and our African teams handle arrival, clearance and final delivery.

How do I get cargo status updates?+

Today by email and messaging, with a dedicated contact per file. A self-service tracking portal is being integrated.
