Project logistics · Destination port

Large mining-truck import & destination-port execution

In this recent large-scale mining-truck transportation project, we acted as the charterer's discharge agent and destination-port transport agent — responsible for on-site execution of the entire logistics chain and overall coordination at the port. The cargo was carried by COSCO SHIPPING Specialized Carriers aboard M/V DU JUAN SONG as a full-vessel shipment; Akita Pacific, as the on-the-ground execution partner, undertook multi-party coordination, discharge organisation, safety supervision and downstream connection at the destination port.

Throughout execution we maintained high-frequency communication with the shipowner and its entire operational team, completing operation plans, resource allocation and risk assessments in advance — achieving efficient, precise and delay-free port operations.

During discharge the team worked strictly to safety standards, delivering multiple large mining trucks damage-free with a smooth operational flow — recognised unanimously by the shipowner, the cargo owner and the port authorities. The project demonstrates Akita Pacific's combined strength in heavy-equipment project logistics, destination-port coordination and multi-party collaboration.
