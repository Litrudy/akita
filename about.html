01Company profile

From one port, to five countries.

Akita Group was founded in Conakry, Guinea in 2019 with a single objective: build the most capable logistics operation in West Africa. Today we run our own fleet, our own bonded warehouses and our own customs teams across five countries.

From COSCO liner calls to 110-ton project cargo, the world's most demanding shippers rely on Akita to move freight where infrastructure is hardest.

With its operating headquarters in Conakry, a coordination desk in Shenzhen and a corporate entity in Hong Kong (HK Akita Pacific Limited), the group connects Chinese and international shippers directly to African operations.

  • Ship-agency licenses in Guinea, Sierra Leone and Congo
  • Priority berthing at 3 key ports
  • Official COSCO designated agent — Guinea
  • Authorized Sailun Tires agent — Guinea
  • Shaanxi Auto official parts warehouse
  • 24/7 cargo visibility & tracking
